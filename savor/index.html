Your recipes stay yours

Each person signs in with their own account. Your book is private to you and the people you invite, and you decide who can add recipes and who can only look.

  • Contributors can add and edit recipes.
  • Viewers can browse and cook, nothing more.
  • Invite links expire and you can turn them off any time.
  • Anyone can leave a book; the owner can remove people.

Add it to your home screen

Savor runs in your browser, so there's nothing to download. Pinning it gives it its own icon, like an app.

iPhone

Open Savor in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. You'll need to sign in once more inside the new icon.

Android

Open Savor in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

Good to know while we're testing

This is an early test, and your feedback is the point. If something is confusing, slow or broken, tell the person who sent you this link. That's exactly what I want to hear.

  • Each free account can import up to 10 recipes a week with Claude, from a link or screenshots. It resets every Monday. Typing in your own recipes is always free and unlimited.
  • Facebook, Instagram and TikTok don't let outsiders read their posts, so Savor builds the recipe from similar published recipes and tells you when it has filled things in. Always give it a quick look before you cook.
  • Savor is run by one person as a small test. Your recipes aren't shown to anyone else in the app, but the person running it can technically reach the database, so please don't store anything sensitive.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

Not during this test. Every account gets 10 recipe imports a week for free. An import is one recipe added with Claude from a link or screenshots, and the draft, the researched version and the photo search all count as one. Typing recipes in yourself, editing, planning meals and sharing are unlimited.

Which sign-in options are there?

Email and password, Google, or Apple. Pick whichever you like; they all give you your own private account.

How do I get my family into my book?

Open your book, tap Share, choose Contributor or Viewer, and send the invite link. They create their own free account and join.

Why does a recipe say it was "filled in"?

When a post doesn't list the ingredients or steps, Savor works them out from similar published recipes and says so, with links to the pages it used. It's a good starting point; adjust amounts to taste.

Can I type in my own recipes?

Yes. Family recipes, cookbook favorites or anything from memory: add them by hand, with your own photo.

How do I remove my account or recipes?

Delete any recipe from its page. To have your whole account removed, tell the person who invited you.
